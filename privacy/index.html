Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

1. Data controller

Lasagne Hub ApS — C/O Kitchen Collective ApS, Slagtehusgade 11A, 1715 Copenhagen V, Denmark. CVR no. 46103289. Email: info@lasagnehub.com. Phone: +45 28 55 58 74.

2. What we collect

When you fill in our quote form, we process what you provide: name, company name, CVR number, email, phone number, preferred date, number of guests, menu preferences and your message.

3. Purpose

To answer your request, prepare a quote, and plan your delivery or event.

4. Legal basis

To take steps at your request prior to a possible agreement (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and/or your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

5. Recipients and processors

We share your data only with the suppliers that help us run the business — our website host and form handler (Netlify / Netlify Forms), our email/IT provider (Google Workspace) and our analytics tool (Google Analytics, once enabled). We have data processing agreements in place. Some providers (including Netlify and Google) are outside the EU/EEA; transfers rely on the EU Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. We never sell your data.

6. Retention

We keep your request as long as needed to handle it and to potentially enter an agreement, then for up to 24 months. If you become a customer, we keep relevant records as required by Danish law (e.g. bookkeeping: 5 years).

7. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. You may withdraw consent at any time. Contact info@lasagnehub.com.

8. Complaints

You may complain to the Danish DPA: Datatilsynet, Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby — dt@datatilsynet.dk — www.datatilsynet.dk.

9. Cookies

We use necessary cookies to make the site work and — only with your consent — cookies for statistics. You give, change or withdraw consent via the cookie banner or the "Cookies" link in the site footer.

  • Necessary — makes the site work and remembers your cookie choice. Cannot be turned off.
  • Statistics — measures visits and usage (e.g. Google Analytics), off until you consent.
  • Marketing — used to show relevant ads. Only active if we run advertising in future.
