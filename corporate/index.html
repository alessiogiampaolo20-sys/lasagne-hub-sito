Questions

Questions

Can we get a fixed weekly lunch day?

Yes, one-off, or a fixed day each week your team looks forward to. Keep your usual lunch, add the day that counts.

Is the catering tax-deductible for companies?

Yes, it's fully tax-deductible for companies.

How many people can you cater for?

Any size, from a small team to a 400-person all-hands, organised case by case.

Do you offer team building with cooking?

Yes, an Italian chef guides your team through building lasagne and tiramisù from scratch. 8–25 in our kitchen, or larger groups at your office or venue.

Can we order individual portions, or only large trays?

Both. Every lasagne and the tiramisù are available as individual portions or as large trays, whichever suits the occasion.
