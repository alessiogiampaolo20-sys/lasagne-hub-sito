For teams

Roll up your sleeves. Build something together.

A hands-on Italian cooking experience where your team makes fresh lasagne and tiramisù from scratch, then sits down to enjoy it together over good wine. Equal parts cooking class, dinner, and the most relaxed team offsite you'll host all year.

Book your team building

No prior skills needed. From the first apron to the last spoon of tiramisù, our chef guides your team through every step. Along the way people loosen up, get their hands messy, compete a little, collaborate a lot, and leave with a shared story (and a full stomach).

Step by step

How the evening unfolds

Eight steps, one unforgettable evening.

1

Welcome & aprons

Everyone gets an apron and a proper welcome glass of wine while the chef walks the team through what the night has in store.

2

Fresh pasta from scratch

Just flour and eggs. Each person kneads their own dough by hand into a smooth ball, and yes, there's a friendly contest for the silkiest, most beautiful dough. The dough then rests in the fridge.

3

Tiramisù cream, together

Now it's teamwork. In small groups, participants whip the eggs and fold in mascarpone to create the heart of the dessert waiting at the end of the night.

4

Roll it out

Back to the pasta. Rolling pin in hand, everyone stretches their dough as thin as they can, the best kind of workout to shake off the workday, and the last effort before the part everyone's been waiting for.

5

Build your lasagne

The chef shows the way, but every plate is personal. Each participant assembles their own portion, layer by layer, free to make it exactly how they like it.

6

Into the oven, and to the table

The lasagne go in to bake while the second half of the experience begins: dinner. Antipasti come out, and a guided wine tasting gets going to accompany the wait.

7

Plated and served

When the lasagne are ready, each one is plated and served, paired with the bottle that best matches the moment.

8

Build your tiramisù

The final act: that mascarpone cream comes back so everyone builds their own tiramisù right at the table, a sweet close to an evening of real relaxation and fun for the whole team.
