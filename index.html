Questions

Questions

What does Lasagne Hub do?

Lasagne Hub is an Italian kitchen in Copenhagen making real lasagne and tiramisù, made fresh, for office catering, team building, company events and private celebrations.

How fast do I get a quote?

Within 12 hours of sending the quote form, no fixed packages, no obligation.

Do you deliver across Copenhagen?

Yes, fresh from our Copenhagen kitchen to offices and private addresses across the city and surrounding areas.

Do you have vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free options?

Yes, a version for everyone at the table, always available: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and dairy-free.

Do you cater for both companies and private events?

Yes. See Corporate for office lunches and team building, or Events for birthdays, dinners and celebrations.

Can I order individual portions, or only large trays?

Both. Every lasagne and the tiramisù are available as individual portions or as large trays, for offices and private events alike.
