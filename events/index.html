Questions

Questions

Can Lasagne Hub cater a birthday party?

Yes, lasagne and tiramisù ready to serve, from a relaxed dinner to a full-blown party.

Do I need to cook or plate anything myself?

No. Food arrives ready to put on the table, so you spend the evening with your guests.

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as you know your date and headcount, you'll get a tailored quote within 12 hours.

Can I order individual portions, or only large trays?

Both. Every lasagne and the tiramisù are available as individual portions or as large trays, depending on your party size.
